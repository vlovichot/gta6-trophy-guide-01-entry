    The road to Vice Got a Price!

    One playthrough, roughly 200 hours by the PowerPyx roadmap's estimate, and only one trophy that can be lost along the way. Work the stages in order: the story first with its single missable watched, strangers and partner feats next, the counted grinds third, free-roam feats and the money loop fourth, and the 100% checklist sweep last. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — GTA VI has no mission replay — and leave cheats off, as launch reporting says they disable trophies.
